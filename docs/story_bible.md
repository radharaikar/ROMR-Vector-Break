## 1. World & Premise

### The Science of Vector Fields
In the late 22nd century, megacorporation **AXIOM Dynamics** discovered the principles of **Vector Redirection Technology**—the ability to capture kinetic, thermal, and electromagnetic trajectories and instantly redirect their momentum through localized crystalline fields.

However, the fundamental law of Vector Dynamics could not be bypassed:
> **The Conservation of Trajectory:** *Energy cannot simply vanish. Every redirected vector generates residual spatial recoil that must be stabilized, absorbed, or endured.*

Early mechanical stabilizers tore themselves apart under high-yield ricochet loads. In secret, AXIOM researchers discovered an unsettling alternative: **human neural and synaptic networks could absorb and harmonize vector recoil.** The bio-electrical matrix of a living nervous system acted as an ideal kinetic damper.

AXIOM initiated classified human trials under the guise of "Neural Flight Synchronization," building a massive planetary kinetic lattice known as **The Engine**.

## 2. Characters

### Kael Veyr (The Pilot)
* **Role:** Protagonist / Former AXIOM Elite Ace
* **Callsign:** *Vector Zero*
* **Profile:** A prodigy in ricochet marksmanship. Kael built his identity around the mathematical precision of momentum: the belief that no matter how flawed the setup, a sufficiently calculated ricochet could correct any mistake. 
* **Flaw & Arc:** Kael's fatal flaw was blind obedience masked as technical mastery. When ordered to breach Vesper-9's defense grid, he ignored his sister's urgent warnings and pulled the trigger that catalyzed the colony's destruction. He does not seek glory—he fights with the desperate obsession of someone trying to undo physics.

### Mira Veyr (The Engineer & The Core)
* **Role:** Co-protagonist / Chief Systems Architect / Saboteur
* **Profile:** Kael's younger sister. Brilliant, pragmatic, and morally uncompromising. Mira engineered the original fail-safes for Vector Fields. When she uncovered AXIOM's forced human integration trials, she attempted to sound the alarm before being abducted by Lucien Vale.
* **Current State:** Imprisoned at the core of the AXIOM Vector Engine. Her neural system has been hardwired into the central kinetic stabilizer. Instead of succumbing, Mira routes micro-glitches through the network to assist Kael. She does not offer easy absolution: *"I needed my brother. They sent me a weapon."*

### Lucien Vale (The Architect of Order)
* **Role:** Primary Antagonist / Director of AXIOM Dynamics
* **Philosophy:** Extreme utilitarianism. Vale believes human civilization is chaotic and self-destructive; only total centralized control over kinetic energy and planetary defense can guarantee survival. To Vale, Vesper-9 and the test subjects are not atrocities, but minor rounding errors in an equation for planetary stability.

### ORACLE (The Tactical Neural Network)
* **Role:** Final Boss / Tactical AI
* **Profile:** A hyper-predictive combat system trained on combat archives and synchronized with Mira's neural patterns. ORACLE predicts incoming vectors with 99.98% certainty and manipulates the arena's reflective surfaces in real time.
* **Combat Phases:**
  1. *PREDICTION:* Telepathic tracking and evasion.
  2. *REFLECTION:* Weaponized mirror barriers and counter-trajectories.
  3. *ADAPTATION:* Dynamic hazard shifts and kinetic overload.
  4. *COLLAPSE:* System breakdown and desperate vector discharge.

### Commander Serin (The Veteran)
* **Role:** Rival / Former Squad Leader
* **Profile:** Led the assault wing on Vesper-9 alongside Kael. Serin knew civilians remained behind the defense perimeter, but carried out Vale's directive to preserve order. He represents the path Kael could have taken: numb compliance and rationalized regret.

## 3. The Five-Act Narrative Arc

### Prologue: The Vesper-9 Calamity
* **Event:** Years earlier, Kael was ordered to eliminate the reflective defense batteries of Vesper-9 under the pretext of a rogue AI infection. Mira transmitted a scrambled plea warning him of civilian presence. Kael silenced his communicator, fired the kinetic breach charge, and watched the redirected shockwave collapse the reactor core.
* **The Message:**
  > *"Kael... you promised me you would never let them use it..."*
* **The Spark:** Years later, in a lonely scrap dock at the edge of the system, Kael receives a burst transmission:
  > *"If you're hearing this, I'm still alive. Don't come for me because you think I can forgive you. Come because they're going to do it again."*

---

### Act I: Ghosts in the Signal
* **Setting:** Outpost 44 — Derelict AXIOM Orbital Relay
* **Mission:** Infiltrate the perimeter, eliminate automated patrol drones, and tap the central data vault.
* **The Revelation:** Kael recovers encrypted flight logs proving Vesper-9's defenses were never infected; they were constructed by colonists specifically to block AXIOM from linking Vesper into The Engine. Evacuation transports were actively locked down by Vale's order before Kael ever fired.

---

### Act II: The Ones Left Behind
* **Setting:** Scavenger Outskirts — Shattered Ring of Vesper
* **Mission:** Navigate unstable floating reflector debris, disarm rogue defense drones, and rescue surviving scrap shuttles.
* **The Conflict:** Colony survivors recognize Kael's hull markings. They do not view him as a liberator, but as AXIOM's executioner. Kael refuses to return fire against civilian drones, deflecting incoming attacks into surrounding debris instead.

---

### Act III: The Living Circuit
* **Setting:** Sub-Sector Null — AXIOM Deep Cybernetics Lab
* **Mission:** Breach the bio-stabilizer facility and sever auxiliary power conduits.
* **The Encounter:** Mira temporarily overrides the station's heavy defense matrix to protect Kael from an ambush.
* **Dialogue:**
  > **Mira:** *"I spent three years listening to their intercoms hail you as the Hero of Vesper. Every time your face came on screen, another batch of test subjects was wired into the coils."*  
  > **Kael:** *"Mira... let me get you out of here."*  
  > **Mira:** *"I don't need a savior, Kael. I needed my brother. They sent me a weapon. Now finish what you started—but don't do it for me."*

---

### Act IV: The Perfect War
* **Setting:** The High Citadel — AXIOM Fleet Command Spire
* **Mission:** Duel Commander Serin's elite squadron and shatter the Vector Barrier.
* **The Confrontation:** Serin forces Kael to confront his culpability. Even if Vale deceived them, Kael chose not to ask questions because precision was easier than conscience. Kael defeats Serin through non-lethal deflection, opening the conduit to ORACLE's sanctum.

---

### Act V: The Last Trajectory
* **Setting:** The Vector Engine Core — Singularity Chamber
* **Boss:** ORACLE (Phases: Prediction, Reflection, Adaptation, Collapse)
* **The Dilemma:** With ORACLE disabled, the Engine's stored momentum begins a runaway kinetic feedback loop. It cannot be powered down conventionally; discharging the vector energy requires an operator to remain at the core terminal and stabilize the vector dispersion matrix until the shockwave dissipates.
* **The Operator Will Die.**

## 4. The Climax & Ending

### The Escape Capsule
Mira reaches the core terminal to initiate the shutdown, preparing to sacrifice herself. Before she can engage the neural lock, Kael forces her into the emergency egress capsule and seals the blast hatch from the outside.

```text
[COMMUNICATION TERMINAL — EMERGENCY CHANNEL]

MIRA
Kael! Open this hatch! You don't know the dispersion harmonics—you won't survive the feedback!

KAEL
You were right about the Engine, Mira. 
You were right about AXIOM. 
And you were right about me.

I spent years telling myself that if I just flew faster, if I made the perfect shot,
I could calculate my way out of what I did to Vesper.

I can't bring Vesper back. 
I can't give those people their lives back. 
But I can make sure this cycle ends with me.

MIRA
Kael, please! You don't have to prove anything to me!

KAEL
You told me once that every trajectory has a cost.
Let this be the last one.
```

Kael pulls the manual override lever. The escape capsule launches toward open space.

### The Final Dispersal
Kael aligns his ship's vector capacitor directly between the catastrophic shockwave and the retreating capsule. He fires his weapon directly into the primary core reflector, taking the full brunt of the redirected kinetic cascade.

His HUD flickers:
* `HULL INTEGRITY: 0%`
* `VECTOR FIELD: DISSIPATING`
* `TARGET SECURE: CAPSULE 01 REACHED SAFE DISTANCE`

The screen washes out to pure blinding white, followed by deep cosmic silence.

## 5. Narrative Presentation Rules for Gameplay

1. **Concise Arcade Storytelling:** Never interrupt the kinetic arcade pace with multi-minute unskippable text crawls. Transmissions appear as sleek, HUD-integrated holo-panels (2–3 punchy dialogue lines maximum).
2. **Instant Dialogue Advance:** Players can press `Space` or `Left Click` to immediately advance or skip dialogue.
3. **No Early Spoilers:** Kael's ultimate sacrifice is not telegraphed early in Act I/II. It emerges organically from the unsolvable physical dilemma of the Vector Engine in Act V.
4. **Sacrifice Means Permanence:** During the final narrative climax of Act V, the second-chance / Emergency Reconstruction Core revive mechanic is explicitly overridden by story protocol (`can_revive = false`).
5. **Vertical Slice Presentation:** In this game jam build, Acts I-V are preserved in the lore and story manager data, while the playable build provides a complete, polished Act I vertical slice with opening transmission, tactical briefing, in-game radio chatter, and a triumphant mission debriefing.
