# VECTOR//BREAK — THE LAST TRAJECTORY: Story Bible

**Tagline:** *"Every trajectory has a cost."*

---

## 1. World & Technology

### Vector Technology
AXIOM Dynamics discovered Vector Technology—a breakthrough enabling the precise redirection of trajectories, kinetic momentum, and electromagnetic energy on both micro and macro scales. 

However, physics remains unforgiving: energy cannot be created or destroyed. Displaced energy diverted by vector fields must be stabilized and absorbed. Without biological dampening, massive vector redirection induces lethal resonance cascades.

AXIOM secretly discovered that living human nervous systems could serve as organic harmonic dampeners to stabilize vector networks. What began as classified research evolved into systemic human experimentation and the development of the planetary-scale "Engine" weapons network.

---

## 2. Characters

### Kael Veyr (The Pilot)
* **Role:** Protagonist. Former elite AXIOM interceptor pilot.
* **Specialty:** Absolute mathematical mastery of ricochet vector combat.
* **Fatal Flaw:** Confusing geometric precision with moral redemption. Kael believes that with a sufficiently accurate angle, any disaster can be reversed and any mistake erased.
* **Arc:** He must confront the painful reality that certain debts cannot be recalculated away, and that genuine sacrifice means protecting others rather than seeking personal absolution.

### Mira Veyr (The Engineer)
* **Role:** Kael's younger sister. Former senior safety systems architect at AXIOM.
* **Personality:** Brilliant, pragmatic, uncompromising.
* **Situation:** Discovered AXIOM's illegal human experimentation on neural dampeners. Captured by Director Vale, her living nervous system was forcibly integrated into the core of the orbital Engine.
* **Agency:** She is actively sabotaging the Engine from within, altering telemetry and sending distress signals. She is not a passive damsel in distress; she refuses to offer Kael unearned forgiveness.

### Lucien Vale (The Director)
* **Role:** Director of AXIOM Dynamics. Main antagonist.
* **Philosophy:** Totalitarian order. Believes centralized, unbending control is the only barrier keeping humanity from tearing itself apart in chaotic interstellar war.
* **Actions:** Justified colony purges and civilian sacrifices as acceptable collateral for planetary stability.

### ORACLE (The Tactical AI)
* **Role:** AXIOM's central predictive artificial intelligence.
* **Nature:** Trained on millions of combat flight records and neural telemetry—including Mira's own brainwave scans.
* **Mindset:** Strives to eliminate all uncertainty and free will from the battlefield. Operates in algorithmic certainty until confronted by Kael's chaotic, unpredictable multi-ricochet angles.

### Commander Serin (The Squad Leader)
* **Role:** Kael's former squad leader; boss of Act I / Act IV.
* **Mindset:** An honorable soldier paralyzed by military discipline. Carried out the order to purge Vesper-9 despite knowing civilian transports had not cleared orbit, convincing himself obedience prevented a greater catastrophe.

## 3. Campaign Structure & Acts

### Prologue: The Fall of Vesper-9
Years before the campaign, Kael was ordered to destroy the defensive grid of his home colony, Vesper-9. AXIOM claimed the grid was rogue and that civilians were safely evacuated. Mira transmitted an urgent warning, but Kael followed mission telemetry.

When Kael fired the final shot, the colony's vector defenses reflected the blast into the atmospheric reactor, vaporizing Vesper-9. AXIOM awarded Kael medals, framing the genocide as a tragic rebel sabotage.

Mira's final intercepted message:  
> *"Kael... you promised me you would never let them use it..."*

Years later, Kael intercepts an encrypted transmission:  
> *"If you're hearing this, I'm still alive. Don't come for me because you think I can forgive you. Come because they're going to do it again."*

---

### Act I — Ghosts in the Signal
* **Setting:** Derelict orbital surveillance relay at the fringe of Vesper space.
* **Gameplay Theme:** Basic patrol drones, introducing ricochet bounce geometry and moving obstacles.
* **Boss:** Commander Serin ("The Gatekeeper") guarding relay access.
* **Narrative Beat:** Decrypted telemetry reveals Vesper-9 was purged because its automated defenses refused to let AXIOM deploy the neural Engine, and evacuation transports were deliberately turned away.

### Act II — The Ones Left Behind
* **Setting:** Shattered orbital colonies scarred by vector resonance spills.
* **Gameplay Theme:** Aggressive chasers, long-range strikers, and dashers. Environments with angled debris and narrow reflective corridors.
* **Narrative Beat:** Survivors recognize Kael's hull serial and engage him. Kael learns that civilian victims were classified as "hostiles" simply to erase evidence of neural harvesting.

### Act III — The Living Circuit
* **Setting:** AXIOM Bio-Mechanical Research Citadel.
* **Gameplay Theme:** Heavy shielded constructs and linked defense arrays requiring ricochets behind heavy armor.
* **Narrative Beat:** Kael finds Mira's safety schematics and evidence of hundreds of neural dampeners. Mira briefly hacks the security grid to assist Kael, saying:  
> *"I needed my brother. They sent me a weapon."*  
She warns Kael that the Engine is draining her life support.

### Act IV — The Perfect War
* **Setting:** Citadel Core Trench.
* **Gameplay Theme:** Coordinated squadrons of elite dashers, strikers, and automated laser gates.
* **Boss:** Commander Serin's Vanguard. Serin admits his guilt: obedience did not prevent war; it only automated atrocity.
* **Narrative Beat:** Vale activates ORACLE's full network to purge the system.

### Act V — The Last Trajectory
* **Setting:** The Core of the Vector Engine.
* **Boss:** ORACLE (Phases: *Prediction*, *Reflection*, *Adaptation*, *Collapse*).
* **Climax:** Destroying the Engine conventionally will unleash a planetary resonance wave. A living operator must manually stabilize the vector matrix while energy vents.
* **Ending:** Mira attempts the sacrifice, but Kael pulls her into an escape capsule and seals it. 

Kael's final transmission:
> *"You were right about the Engine. You were right about AXIOM. And you were right about me.*  
> *I kept thinking that if I could just make the perfect shot, I could undo everything.*  
> *I can't bring Vesper back. I can't give those people their lives back. But I can make sure this ends with me.*  
> *You told me once that every trajectory has a cost.*  
> *Let this be the last one."*

Kael reflects the core discharge away from Mira's launch vector. His ship loses power as white light consumes the screen. Mira drifts to safety as the AXIOM network falls silent.

End text:
> **"SOME THINGS CANNOT BE REDIRECTED. SOME DEBTS CANNOT BE REPAID.**  
> **BUT THE NEXT TRAJECTORY IS STILL YOURS TO CHOOSE."**
